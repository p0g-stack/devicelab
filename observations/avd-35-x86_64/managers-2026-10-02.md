## 19:10 — metamodule correction

Hybrid Mount is not a non-working metamodule: it mounts demo v0.1.39's app on a real boot (Yuv's phone, KernelSU Next,
Hybrid Mount 4.2.0 magic mode). The lab could not get it to mount (managers-2026-10-01.md)

emulator kernel; `ksud late-load` only), so metamodules that mount early (post-fs-data before system starts) are not
testable here; a real-boot leg would need an emulator kernel with KernelSU built in. Re-run 19:25 on v0.1.39 (KernelSU and

the module, but nothing is mounted on /product/app and it leaves no log (runs `runs/*v0.1.39-meta-hybrid-*`).

## 19:10 — metamodule correction

Hybrid Mount is not a non-working metamodule: it mounts demo v0.1.39's app on a real boot (Yuv's phone, KernelSU Next,
Hybrid Mount 4.2.0 magic mode). The lab's "not mounted" result (managers-2026-10-01.md) was not reproducible-as-working
on the AVD's late-load boot. Lab limitation: the AVD cannot run KernelSU at boot (release LKM does not insmod on the
emulator kernel; `ksud late-load` only), so metamodules that mount early (post-fs-data before system starts) are not
testable here; a real-boot leg would need an emulator kernel with KernelSU built in.
